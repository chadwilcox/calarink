Clip rules & privacy

  • Upload only clips you have the right to share. No TV or streaming broadcasts.
  • Get permission from the people featured, and from a parent or guardian for anyone under 18. Leave out full names of kids.
  • Hockey plays only: no fights, injuries, or anything meant to embarrass someone.
  • Want a clip removed? Use Report on the clip. You can delete your own clips on this page any time.
  • You keep ownership of your clip. Uploading lets Calarink show it here.
  • We keep uploaders' emails, and a scrambled form of voters' and reporters' network addresses to keep voting fair. A cookie remembers your votes. None of it is shown or sold.
  • Clips can include sound from the rink. Leave it out if it has recorded music or private conversations.
  • Location information is removed from clips before upload. Each week's top 10 stay; other clips are deleted about 30 days after their week ends.
